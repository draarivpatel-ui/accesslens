AccessLens is a local document workbench that turns dense school handouts and inaccessible HTML into a reading copy, an audit tied to the source and a study deck. Everything runs inside this browser tab, with no uploads, no external processing and no document storage. All 388 tests pass, and 33 headless browser checks pass at two viewports with zero console errors and zero external requests.

Load the Club page HTML example, a fictional flyer for a design club build night. Run the checks, and the analysis board lists 13 findings, from a missing page title to weak contrast, an unclear link and unlabeled controls. Open the low contrast finding and press Show in source, and the exact characters in the pasted HTML are selected in the editor.
