        How AccessLens works

        Scores

        • Flesch-Kincaid, SMOG and Gunning Fog estimate a US school grade. Lower estimates suggest easier reading. The Dale-Chall proxy is a separate difficulty score.
        • Difficult-word share is the fraction of words outside a small common vocabulary list. Long-word share counts the 3+ syllable estimate.
        • Every estimate is a heuristic check on the text. None of them measure real comprehension.
        • Flag counts are limited checks and signals, not a full WCAG conformance audit.

        Key commands

        • Turn on Key commands, then press r to run checks and e to jump to the editor.
        • Keys never fire while you are typing, while a control has focus, or while a modifier key is held.

        WCAG audit

        • The audit table compares original and reading copy on published readability equations.
        • Brick histogram bars mark sentences over 25 words.
        • Contrast badges use exact sRGB relative luminance on the export palette.
        • Preview profiles test dyslexia spacing, high contrast and the screen-reader outline.

        Exports

        • Reading page .html: the built copy as a standalone readable page.
        • Cards .tsv: deck rows ready for Anki import.
        • Audit .csv: stats and findings in one sheet.
        • Bundle .json: the full report including your pasted source. Review it before sharing.
        • Study page .html: a printable study sheet.
        • Review .csv: your ratings with the next review dates.
        • Certificate .json/.csv/.md: the signed audit report. Paste a .json into the verify box to check it.

        Privacy

        • Everything runs in this tab. No network calls, no storage, no uploads.
        • Ratings live in memory only, until you export them.
        • The reading copy is a suggested edit. Your source text is never changed.

        AccessLens runs entirely in this browser tab. Text you paste never leaves the page.
